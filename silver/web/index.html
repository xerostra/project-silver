Fair price is what an item actually trades at: the volume-weighted median of its daily average sale price over the last 14 days (28 if it rarely trades), across the city markets, at Normal quality, with troll-priced days (over 4× off the typical day) dropped first. Items that sold on only a day or two can still be off, so they're hidden by default. Ask vs fair is today's cheapest listing within the max price age against that fair price, so a big negative number is an item listed well under what it normally sells for. Per seed or baby. Crops return 3–6 (6–12 with premium); watering adds seed-return chance, and over 100% means a chance of a second seed. Animals eat the cheapest accepted food (every crop is 48 nutrition, meat 52); focused feedings add offspring chance, and exotic mounts can only be bred with focus. Top: buy now (cheapest sell order). Bottom: sell now (best buy order). Hearts drop from faction warfare, so each one is usually cheapest in its own faction's city.

Worth spending on refining?

Every T4–T8 refine can swap one heart for one raw resource — the same single heart whether it saves a T4 ore or a T8.3 one. So a heart is only worth using where the raw it replaces costs more than the heart. Raw prices are capped at their 7-day average so a troll listing can't fake a saving. Return rate applies to the heart and the raw alike, so it doesn't change which is cheaper.

Shadowheart conversion

Shadowheart converts 1:1 into any other heart for , which caps what any heart is worth: nobody pays more than a Shadowheart plus the fee.

Faction transports

Take a mission at your faction's store, pay your own hearts, carry the pack to a rival's smuggling agent, swap it, and bring theirs back for their hearts. The amounts are fixed by the game: each store offers missions for set distance bands (e.g. a heavy pack costs 15 hearts and pays 18 at 4–8 zones up to 22 at 10–12; 21 to or from Caerleon). Zones and risk are for the shortest path; hearts are valued at what they'd sell for (7-day average, as a sell order). Hover a row for the path.

Buy an item, upgrade it in your inventory, sell it. No station is involved, so each part is bought wherever it's cheapest right now (within the max price age) and the result sold wherever pays best: instantly into a buy order, the Black Market included, or listed as a sell order at the lower of today's ask and the city's fair price. Jumps over several levels (.0 → .3) are included; they can pay when no single step does. Typical is the same trade at fair prices: a big "now" with a poor "typical" is usually a stale or lucky price. Volumes are thin for most enchanted gear, so check how many sell a day. Hover a row for every part.
